# CL-MCSDS-CMU-10-04_DAS7003_ls-lrsi-aranayake
A location-specific landslide risk index for Aranayake, Kegalle District, Sri Lanka. Combines terrain, vegetation, rainfall, proximity to roads/streams, soil moisture, and satellite-based ground deformation (InSAR) into one weighted risk score on a 30 m grid, checked against the 2016 Aranayake landslide saite.
